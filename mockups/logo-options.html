Quantrivia — logo options

Three directions in the Midnight Slate palette. Each one shows a full lockup, the mark on its own, and the mark at app-icon size.

A · Question Q

The question mark turned into a Q, so the name and the game's job read at once. The blue dot is the answer. Works best on dark backgrounds.

B · Closeness meter

The three closeness tiers from the game (green, yellow, and the blue answer) as a bar. It explains the game in the logo itself. Works on light and dark.

C · Stat tile

A bold yellow tile with a number sign, a nod to the game's numeric guesses. It's the simplest of the three and the easiest to recognise at small sizes.
