Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Quantrivia is a numbers trivia game run by Michael Moran as an individual project. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and what choices you have. It is written in plain language. If you have questions, contact contact@playquantrivia.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: the username you choose, and a password. Passwords are stored only as a bcrypt hash, never in plain text.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and email address. We use them to link your account. We don't receive your Google password.
  • Game data: your match results, guesses made during matches, ratings, friend connections, and challenge invitations.
  • Reports: if you report another player, we keep the report and the details you give us, so we can review it.
  • Technical data: your IP address, used to limit repeated sign-up, sign-in, and message attempts. Those limits are kept in the server's memory and reset when it restarts.
  • Answer statistics: in multiplayer matches, we record each answer's question and how close the guess was. These records aren't linked to your account, and they are used only to judge how fair each question is.
  • Session cookie: a cookie that keeps you signed in. The game needs it to work, so it is not optional while you are signed in.

How we use it

  • To run your account and the game, including matches, rankings, and friends.
  • To send password reset emails, when you ask for one.
  • To review reports and keep the game fair.
  • To protect the service against abuse, such as spam sign-ups or flooding.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Other players see your username, rating, and match results, as well as your profile if you're in a game with them.
  • Google, if you use Google sign-in, for the sign-in itself.
  • Our email provider, which delivers password reset and report-related emails. Only the details needed to send the email are shared.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server where the data is stored.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data while your account is active. Reset links expire after one hour and can be used only once. Reports are kept while they are being reviewed and for a reasonable time afterwards.

Your choices

  • Access and correction: you can see your profile and match history in the game. To ask for a copy of your data or a correction, email us.
  • Deletion: email us to delete your account. We will remove your account and personal details, though some match records may be anonymized rather than deleted so other players' results remain accurate.
  • Google: you can stop using Google sign-in by deleting your account, and you can remove the connection in your Google account settings.

Children

Quantrivia is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and limits on repeated requests. No online service is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee that information will never be accessed without permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
